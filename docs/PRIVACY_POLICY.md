# Vivra Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Vivra is a personal health journal for iPhone. It's built so that your data stays
with you. This policy explains what Vivra does with your information.

## The short version

- **Vivra doesn't collect your data.** There's no account and no server. The app
  makes no network connections of its own.
- **Everything stays on your iPhone,** in an encrypted database.
- **Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.**
- **You can export or delete everything at any time,** from Settings in the app.

## What Vivra reads

With your permission, Vivra reads data from Apple Health so it can show your trends.
You choose which kinds of data to allow, and you can change your choices at any time
in the iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Vivra. The kinds of data
Vivra can ask for are:

- **Sleep:** sleep stages and time in bed.
- **Heart:** heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, walking heart
  rate, VO2 max, ECG recordings and irregular rhythm notifications. ECG recordings
  and notifications are listed as Apple Health recorded them, never interpreted.
- **Activity:** steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, walking and
  running distance, and stand hours.
- **Workouts:** type, duration, energy and distance. Vivra doesn't read workout
  routes or locations.
- **Mind:** State of Mind entries and mindful minutes.
- **Nutrition and hydration:** food energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, water,
  caffeine and alcoholic drinks.
- **Body:** weight, body fat, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and sleeping wrist
  temperature.

Vivra also stores what you log in the app: mood, energy, stress, drinks, meals and
notes. It also stores your settings, such as a daily goal, a reminder time and, if
you set one, a birth year for comparisons with published reference data.

## What Vivra writes

Only if you turn it on in Settings, Vivra saves the mood and drinks you log to Apple
Health, so other apps you allow can use them. Energy, stress, meals and notes stay in
Vivra. If you delete an entry in Vivra, Vivra also deletes it from Apple Health.

## Where your data is kept

Your data is stored only on your iPhone:

- in a database encrypted with a key that's kept in your iPhone's Keychain and never
  leaves the device;
- with iOS Data Protection, so the files can't be read while your iPhone is locked.

Vivra doesn't store Health data in iCloud, and it isn't included in any service run
by the developer, because there isn't one.

You can turn on **App Lock** in Settings to require Face ID or your passcode to open
Vivra.

## Summaries and Apple Intelligence

Vivra's statistics are all calculated on your iPhone. On devices with Apple
Intelligence turned on, Vivra uses Apple's on-device language model to word its
summaries. That processing happens on your iPhone, and Vivra doesn't send your data
to Apple or anyone else for it. Without Apple Intelligence, Vivra writes summaries
from built-in templates.

## Sharing

Vivra doesn't share, sell or rent your information, and doesn't use it for
advertising, marketing or data mining. It contains no analytics, advertising or
tracking software.

Data leaves Vivra only when you choose to export it (see below), and it goes only
where you send it.

## Exporting your data

Settings › **Export All Data** creates a file containing everything Vivra has stored,
as JSON and spreadsheet (CSV) files. It opens the iPhone's share sheet so you can
choose where to save or send it. Once you share it, that copy is outside Vivra's
encryption, so choose its destination with care. Vivra deletes its temporary copy
when the share sheet closes.

## Deleting your data

Settings › **Delete All Vivra Data** deletes Vivra's database, its encryption key and
its settings from your iPhone. Deleting the app has the same effect. Anything you
chose to save to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Vivra schedules it on your iPhone. No reminder
data is sent anywhere.

## Crash reports

Vivra has no crash reporting of its own. If you've chosen to share analytics with
app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics &
Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with the developer. These don't include
your Vivra data. You control this setting.

## Children

Vivra isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect
information from anyone. Its reference comparisons use adult data and are offered
only for ages 18 and over.

## Not medical advice

Vivra describes patterns in your own data. It isn't a medical device and doesn't
provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
